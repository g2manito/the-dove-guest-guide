Arrive

Your first five minutes.

🔑 Entry

Your private entry code is sent through your booking-platform arrival message. Both exterior locks auto-lock after about five minutes.

🚗 Parking

Park up to four vehicles in The Dove driveway. Please avoid grass, neighboring property and nearby access.

📶 Wi-Fi

DoveNetGuest

For privacy, access codes and Wi-Fi passwords are never published in this public guide.

Settle in

Make yourself at home.

☕ Coffee & tea

Nespresso VertuoPlus and Fellow Aiden Precision Coffee Maker, plus starter coffee, tea, sweeteners and creamer.

📺 Entertainment

Roku TVs use Guest Mode. Choose your checkout date when signing in so Roku signs you out automatically. Books, cards and a puzzle are available too.

🧺 Laundry

Washer, dryer and liquid detergent are available. At checkout, used towels go in the laundry-room hamper.

🛏️ Bedrooms

Each bedroom has a charging station on one nightstand. Extra bedding is in the bedroom closets.

🌡️ Temperature

Ecobee is generally around 72°F cooling / 70°F heating. Adjust within the available range for comfort.

🍽️ Microwave & septic

Press the LG microwave release button before pulling the door. Flush only toilet paper; put wipes and other products in the trash.

Enjoy

The best parts of staying in.

♨️ Private hot tub

The 7-person Hot Spring Limelight Pulse is on the lower deck and is generally set to 100°F for arrival. Maximum temperature is 104°F.

  • Maximum 7 people
  • Children under 5 may not use the spa
  • Guests under 18 must be actively supervised
  • No glass, food or bath products
  • Rinse off sunscreen, lotion, oils and makeup first
  • Avoid use while impaired
  • Near 104°F, about 15–20 minutes is a sensible soak

Hot-tub towels: front entry closet.

When finished, turn off jets and lights, replace and secure the cover, and bring towels inside.

If water looks unusually cloudy, discolored or foamy—or has a strong chemical odor—do not use the spa; contact your host through the booking platform.

🔥 Solo Stove

Use supplied firewood and starters for a modest fire. Keep the spark screen in place. Never leave an active fire unattended. Let flames burn down completely before placing the metal lid over the stove.

🍔 Grill

Open lid and propane valve, ignite, cook, brush warm grates, turn burners off and close propane. A full spare tank is in the garage.

⚡ EV charging

Tesla Universal Wall Connector in the garage supports Tesla and compatible non-Tesla EVs. Guest charging is complimentary.

Safety & house rules

Simple things to know.

Fire & CO

Combination smoke/CO alarms are installed in every bedroom and main living areas. Fire extinguishers: laundry room, garage worktable and upper deck.

Severe weather

For a tornado warning, move to the interior hallway near the garage/laundry room, away from windows and exterior doors.

Outdoors

The backyard has slopes and uneven terrain. Supervise children. The nearby pond is viewing-only.

  • No smoking or vaping anywhere on the property
  • No pets
  • Quiet hours: 10 PM–8 AM
  • No parties or events; small celebrations are welcome within occupancy, parking and quiet-hour limits
  • Primary booking guest must be at least 25
  • Only registered guests may stay overnight
Leave

Checkout is 10:00 AM.

There's no long chore list. We simply ask that you:

  1. Load and start the dishwasher.
  2. Place used towels in the laundry-room hamper.
  3. Remove personal food and beverages from refrigerator, freezer and pantry.
  4. Tie trash bags and place them in the lidded garbage can in the garage.
  5. Turn off interior lights and ceiling fans.
  6. Make sure exterior doors are fully closed.

No need to strip beds, start laundry or change the thermostat.
